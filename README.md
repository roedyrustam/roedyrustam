## 🌌 About Me

I am a passionate software engineer and system builder focused on **Autonomous AI Agent Swarms**, **High-Availability AI Gateways**, **Domain-Driven SaaS**, and **Open Data Infrastructure**. 

My work spans from building developer-first tooling for frontier AI ecosystems (Google Antigravity, Claude Code, Cursor) to domain-specific enterprise platforms like digital coffee cupping standards (**SCA 2025 CVA**) and national open datasets.

- ☕ Crafting digital solutions for the specialty coffee industry with **CuppingNotes** & **BeansHub**.
- 📍 Based in Indonesia 🇮🇩 | Building for developers worldwide 🌍.
